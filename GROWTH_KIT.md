# Gilded Eye Growth Kit

> Draft assets for quiet launch. Requires alt account (not main).

## Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### ICP 1: Indie Founders
- Building solo or small team (1–5 people)
- Making product, hiring, or funding decisions monthly
- Already spends time in founder communities (Indie Hackers, X, Discord)
- Pain point: "decision fatigue" after context-switching all day

### ICP 2: Senior ICs (Product, Eng, Design)
- 5+ years experience, individual contributor track
- Evaluating job offers, side projects, career pivots
- Makes high-stakes decisions without a team to debate with
- Pain point: overthinking, analysis paralysis on big moves

### ICP 3: Your Graph (Warm)
- Already follows you or interacts with your content
- Has mentioned "decisions," "career," "strategy" in posts
- Uses Notion, Obsidian, or task boards
- Most likely to buy — low trust barrier

1. **Create alt account** (not your main)
2. **Warm it up** — 10–15 non-promotional posts over 1–2 weeks
3. **Post 1 thread** without linking (builds credibility)
4. **Then** start sending DMs and linking
